ARTWORKS

yuvraj argal

I draw faces at the moment they stop performing. Watercolour, ink, alcohol markers and charcoal on paper — usually finished in one sitting, because the second sitting is where the honesty goes.

The titles tend to arrive before the work does. Somewhere Between Trust and Truth. Nobody Told Him He Could Cry. They aren't descriptions of what you're looking at; they're the thing I was trying to hold still long enough to paint.

Alongside the paintings I work as an art director, building identities and visual systems for studios and brands. The two feed each other more than they compete — the discipline of a system keeps the loose work deliberate, and the loose work keeps the systems from going cold.

Everything in the shop is an original. One of one, signed, framed and sent from Italy. Each piece exists once; if one finds you, there isn't another of it anywhere.

Commissions, exhibitions and studio visits — hey@yuvistark.com.
